# Call for Papers

We invite submissions of **4-page extended abstracts** (NeurIPS format, excluding references) covering any of the workshop topics. Accepted papers will be **non-archival**.

## Submission Topics

- Traffic sign/signal detection and map element association
- Visual inference of road attributes (speed limits, lane counts, surface type)
- Map change detection from visual observations
- Vision-language models for geospatial and topological reasoning
- Graph neural networks for road network modeling
- Scene graph to HD map alignment
- Multi-modal sensor-to-map conflation
- Self-supervised learning from fleet-scale unlabeled driving data
- Benchmarks and evaluation protocols for map extraction
- Foundation models for geospatial understanding

## Submission Guidelines

- **Format:** NeurIPS 2026 workshop paper format (4 pages + references)
- **Review:** Single-blind review process
- **Presentation:** ~8 papers selected for oral presentation, ~20 for posters
- **Archival status:** Non-archival (can be submitted elsewhere)
